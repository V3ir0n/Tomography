About NOVAC
"About NOVAC: The Network for Observation of Volcanic and Atmospheric Change is a global collaboration between volcano observatories and research institutions that advances the monitoring and scientific understanding of volcanic emissions and their impacts."
Sustained By
"The joint effort of volcano observatories, Chalmers University of Technology, and the Volcano Disaster Assistance Program of the United States Geological Survey. Established through funding from the European Commission, the International Geoscience Programme of UNESCO/IUGS, the Swedish Space Agency, the Swedish Research Council and other funding sources over the years."
